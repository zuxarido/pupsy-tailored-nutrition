1. Document Overview
This document defines all website requirements for Pupsy -- a tech-enabled personalised fresh dog food subscription brand operating in India. It is intended for the design and development team and covers every page, user flow, feature, and screen state needed to build the first version of the Pupsy website. Operational, logistics, and kitchen processes are explicitly out of scope.

Project
Pupsy Website -- v1.0
Scope
Marketing site + subscription sign-up + customer account portal
Out of Scope
Mobile apps, kitchen ops software, delivery routing, internal admin panel
Primary Audience
Urban Indian dog owners, ages 24–45, smartphone-first

3. Page-by-Page Requirements
3.1  Home Page  (/)
The home page is the primary conversion page. Its job is to communicate the Pupsy proposition in under 10 seconds and funnel visitors into the questionnaire. Every section should build trust and urgency.

Hero Section
	•	Full-bleed visual: a happy dog eating from a Pupsy-branded meal pack, warm kitchen-morning feel
	•	Headline (approx): "Real food. Cooked fresh. At your door every morning."
	•	Sub-headline: "Personalised daily meals for your dog -- delivered like a dabbawala, every single day."
	•	Primary CTA button: Build [Name]s Plan -- opens questionnaire
	•	Secondary CTA: 'See How It Works' -> scrolls or links to /how-it-works
	•	Trust bar below hero: 3–4 icons with short copy -- e.g., 'Vet-Formulated', 'Fresh Every Morning', 'No Preservatives', 'Portioned for Your Dog'

The Problem Section
	•	Short copy block explaining what's wrong with kibble, frozen food, and generic pet food
	•	Simple 2–3 column comparison (kibble / frozen / Pupsy) -- NOT a table-heavy layout, more visual

How Pupsy Works  (Summary)
	•	3-step visual strip: Tell Us About Your Dog -> We Cook Fresh Every Morning -> Delivered to Your Door
	•	Each step has an icon, a headline, and one line of copy
	•	CTA: 'Get Started' -> questionnaire

The Freshness Story
	•	Photo-heavy section showing real ingredients: chicken, rice, vegetables
	•	Short copy about no preservatives, human-grade ingredients, cooked daily in a FSSAI-licensed kitchen
	•	Key differentiator callout: 'Unlike frozen food that has to be thawed -- yours is cooked this morning.'

Recipe Tease Section
	•	3–4 recipe cards in a horizontal scroll or grid
	•	Each card: recipe name, hero ingredient image, 2-line description
	•	No pricing on cards -- CTA links to /recipes or questionnaire

Social Proof
	•	3–5 customer reviews with dog name, owner name, city, star rating, quote
	•	Optional: before/after energy/coat improvement anecdote
	•	Trust logos: FSSAI, Vet Partner (if applicable), any media mentions

Final CTA Strip
	•	Strong colour background strip (brand orange)
	•	Headline: 'Your dog deserves better. Start today.'
	•	CTA button: 'Build [Dog Name]'s Plan'

Footer
	•	Logo, tagline
	•	Navigation links
	•	Social media icons (Instagram, Facebook)
	•	Legal: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy
	•	Contact email / WhatsApp number


3.2  How It Works  (/how-it-works)
Explains the full customer journey and the daily delivery model. This is a critical trust-building page for first-time visitors who don't understand the concept.

	•	Section 1: The Setup -- You answer questions about your dog -> we generate a personalised meal plan -> you subscribe
	•	Section 2: Morning Cooking -- Every morning, Pupsy's kitchen cooks fresh batches based on the day's orders
	•	Section 3: The Delivery -- Two packs delivered each morning: one for morning feeding, one goes into the fridge for evening feeding
	•	Section 4: Feeding Time -- Owner opens the pack, no thawing, no measuring -- just serve
	•	Include an illustrated or animated timeline graphic showing the full day loop
	•	FAQ accordion at the bottom (see FAQ section requirements)

💡 Note: This page should address the key concern: 'How is this different from frozen food?' The answer -- it's cooked today, not frozen, not retort-packaged -- needs to be explicit and repeated.

	•	Visual grid of raw ingredients: chicken breast, brown rice, spinach, carrots, turmeric, etc.
	•	Short copy on sourcing: local, seasonal, no fillers, no by-products

3.4  Dog Profile Questionnaire  (/get-started)
This is the most important feature on the website. It is the primary conversion mechanism. The questionnaire should feel warm, personalised, and quick. Each step should be a single screen -- no multi-question pages.

General UX Rules for the Questionnaire
	•	One question per screen -- full-page transitions between steps
	•	Dog's name used throughout once entered: 'Great! Now tell us about [Name]'s breed'
	•	Progress bar visible at the top across all steps
	•	Back button always visible and functional
	•	Answers saved to session state; if user refreshes, show a 'Continue where you left off?' prompt
	•	Mobile-first layout -- large tap targets, thumb-friendly
	•	No login/signup wall until after the recommendation is shown
	•	Estimated time shown at the start: 'Takes 2 minutes'

Step 10 -- The Results Screen
This is the payoff moment. Design it to feel like a reveal.
	•	Animated transition into results: 'We've built [Name]'s plan!'
	•	Recommended recipe card (1 primary, 1 alternate shown as 'also works for [Name]')
	•	Daily portion size shown clearly: e.g., '280g per day -- 2 packs of 140g'
	•	Price shown for the first time: e.g., '₹149/day -- delivered every morning'
	•	Breakdown of why this recipe was chosen based on the dog's answers
	•	CTA: 'Start [Name]'s Plan' -> leads to checkout / account creation
	•	Secondary: 'Change Recipe' toggle -- shows alternate options
💡 Note: Even though Pupsy has 3–4 fixed recipes, the results screen must feel personalised. Use the dog's name, their specific weight, and a 1-sentence personalisation note to make it feel custom.


3.5  Checkout  (/checkout)
Triggered after the user clicks 'Start [Name]'s Plan' on the results screen. For v1.0, this can be a simple form + Razorpay/Cashfree payment integration. Keep it 2 steps maximum.

Step 1 -- Account Creation + Delivery Details
	•	Name, email, phone number (OTP verification)
	•	Delivery address with PIN code check (show error if outside delivery zone)
	•	Preferred delivery time slot if applicable
	•	Order summary sidebar: recipe, dog name, daily price, subscription cadence

Step 2 -- Payment
	•	Razorpay or Cashfree integration (standard for India)
	•	UPI, net banking, card, and wallet options
	•	Auto-renewal consent checkbox with clear language
	•	First delivery date shown explicitly: 'Your first delivery: Tomorrow morning'
	•	Discount code field (optional for v1)

Order Confirmation Screen
	•	Big friendly confirmation: 'Woof! [Name]'s meals are confirmed! 🎉'
	•	Summary: recipe, first delivery date, daily portion, price
	•	What to expect next: 'We'll cook [Name]'s meal tomorrow morning and deliver it by [time]'
	•	Email confirmation auto-sent
	•	CTA: 'Go to My Account'

3.6  Customer Account Portal  (/account)
Post-login area for existing subscribers to manage their plan and view delivery info.

Dashboard
	•	Greeting: 'Good morning! [Name]'s meal is being cooked right now 🍲'
	•	Next delivery card: date, time, recipe
	•	Quick links: Manage Plan, Track Delivery, Edit Dog Profile

Manage Plan
	•	Current recipe with 'Swap Recipe' button -- shows other 2–3 options
	•	Pause subscription: choose pause duration (1 week / 2 weeks / custom date)
	•	Resume subscription
	•	Cancel subscription -- with a retention flow asking reason, offering a discount or pause first
	•	Change delivery address

Delivery Schedule
	•	Calendar or list view of upcoming deliveries
	•	Status labels: Scheduled / Being Prepared / Out for Delivery / Delivered
	•	Skip a day functionality: tap any upcoming date to skip it

Order History
	•	List of past deliveries: date, recipe, status, invoice link

Dog Profile
	•	Edit any field from the original questionnaire
	•	Re-run questionnaire to update meal recommendation if dog's profile changes significantly

Account Settings
	•	Update personal info: name, email, phone
	•	Change password
	•	Update saved payment method
	•	Notification preferences: WhatsApp / email / SMS for delivery updates

5. Non-Functional Requirements

5.1  Performance
	•	Page load time under 3 seconds on a 4G mobile connection
	•	Time to interactive (TTI) under 4 seconds
	•	Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, FID) must be in 'Good' range for Google Search ranking

5.2  SEO
	•	All public pages must have unique, editable meta titles and descriptions
	•	Structured data markup (Schema.org) on recipe pages and FAQ page
	•	Sitemap.xml auto-generated and submitted to Google Search Console
	•	Blog articles must be fully crawlable and indexable

5.3  Security
	•	HTTPS across all pages (SSL mandatory)
	•	OTP-based authentication -- no plain text password storage
	•	PCI-DSS compliance handled by payment gateway (Razorpay / Cashfree) -- no card data stored on Pupsy servers
	•	GDPR/DPDP Act-compliant data handling and cookie consent banner

5.4  Accessibility
	•	Minimum WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
	•	All images must have descriptive alt text
	•	Keyboard-navigable forms in the questionnaire
	•	Sufficient colour contrast for all text and CTA elements

5.5  Analytics & Tracking
	•	Google Analytics 4 installed on all pages
	•	Conversion events tracked: Questionnaire Started, Questionnaire Completed, Plan Selected, Purchase Completed
	•	Funnel drop-off tracking per questionnaire step
	•	Meta Pixel installed for Facebook/Instagram ad retargeting


6. Design Guidelines

The visual language should feel warm, fresh, and trustworthy -- not clinical. Think: Sunday morning farmers market, not sterile pet pharmacy.

6.1  Tone
	•	Warm, conversational, dog-parent-first
	•	Copy always talks to the owner as someone who loves their dog, not as a consumer
	•	Avoid overly technical nutrition language in the UI -- save it for the blog
	•	Playful but not childish -- this is a premium product

6.2  Visual Direction
	•	Photography: real dogs, real food, natural light, Indian homes. Avoid stock-photo corporate imagery.
	•	Colour palette: warm whites, muted orange, earthy greens, cream backgrounds
	•	Typography: clean sans-serif (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, or similar)
	•	Illustrations: optional hand-drawn-style icons to add warmth
	•	Icons: consistent icon set throughout -- do not mix styles

6.3  Key UI Components
	•	Floating CTA button on mobile (always visible 'Build My Dog's Plan')
	•	Sticky header on desktop with logo and primary CTA
	•	WhatsApp chat bubble -- always visible, bottom right
	•	Recipe cards -- consistent card component used across /our-food, /recipes, and questionnaire results
	•	Progress stepper -- used in questionnaire and checkout

The following must all be true before the website goes live to first customers:

	•	Questionnaire fully functional -- all 10 steps, session persistence, mobile-tested
	•	Recipe recommendation logic implemented and verified against all input combinations
	•	Checkout with Razorpay live (not test mode)
	•	Order confirmation email working
	•	Account portal: plan management + delivery schedule working
	•	PIN code delivery area validation live
	•	All marketing pages live: Home, Our Food, How It Works, Recipes, FAQ, About
	•	Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Refund Policy pages published
	•	Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel installed and conversion events verified
	•	WhatsApp chat button live with active Pupsy business number
	•	SSL certificate active, site loads on HTTPS
	•	Mobile responsiveness tested on iPhone SE, iPhone 14, Android mid-range
	•	End-to-end user journey tested by at least 3 real dog owners (non-team)
